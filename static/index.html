Run the pipeline

Two steps, run in order. Each button explains what it does below it.

1
Pulls your last 90 days of Search Console data (blog pages only) and adds two kinds of topic to the backlog below: pages getting clicks but no impressions boost (REVIVAL), and real searches with no strong page yet (GAP). Doesn't write anything - just fills the queue.
2
Takes whatever's queued in the backlog (up to your per-run limit) and, for each one, runs: SEO research → draft → fact-check → humanize pass → SEO metadata. Saves as a WordPress draft - never publishes automatically.
